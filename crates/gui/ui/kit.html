Drag to reorder; the top part is picked first by a click. The number is the Front depth: parts at one depth merge into one surface, a higher Front stands out (lower parts draw darker).
Click selects a part, drag moves it, corner handles scale (Shift keeps the proportions), the handle above rotates, arrows nudge. Double-click a part to edit its outline point by point. Wheel zooms, middle drag or Space+drag pans. The solid square is the note box: whatever a part puts outside it is cut off, nothing is ever rescaled. The dashed square is the game's true hit area. Parts at the same depth merge into one surface, in the game too; a part with a higher Front stands out and lower parts draw darker here. 3D shows the note turned. Help lists every control. Save writes one .rhs file: the skin for rhythr and the game, with your editable design inside, so Open brings it back here. Import that file into the game from a local folder.
